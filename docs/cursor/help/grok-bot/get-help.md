# Get help

Contact support when you need help with Grok Bot.

## How do I contact Grok Bot support?

Email [**hi@cursor.com**](mailto:hi@cursor.com).

## What should I include in a support request?

- The email on your Grok Bot account.
- Grok Bot version and platform: Mac, Windows, or iOS.
- What you were trying to do.
- A screenshot of the error or plan screen.
- What troubleshooting steps you have tried.

## What if my question is about billing or cancellation?

Email [**hi@cursor.com**](mailto:hi@cursor.com) and provide a quick summary of the issue. We will be in contact as soon as possible.
